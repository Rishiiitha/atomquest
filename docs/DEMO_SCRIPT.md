# Demo script

## Short walkthrough

1. Start on **Twin**. Pick a scenario and inspect the live readings and stage health.
2. Open **Why is flow down?**. Increase TDS and show how raw permeate flow can fall while normalized permeability remains stable; then change membrane permeability to show true fouling.
3. Open **Evidence**. Compare P1 with raw and counter baselines at fixed horizons. Point out SIMULATED labels and the OOD row.
4. Open **Home concept**. Show a stage replacement window and repeat the water-safety disclaimer.
5. Open **Bench readiness**. Explain current-to-pressure calibration, pressure ground truth, and the CSV adapter.
6. End on **Limits**. State that no household purifier data or bench measurements were available for this prototype.

## Extended walkthrough

Use the same route order, then inspect fleet policy sensitivity, telemetry tiers, the signature matrix, and the bench protocol. Let judges challenge pressure observability and attribution. Treat the answers in `docs/CLAIMS_REGISTER.md` as the source of truth.

## Judge questions and honest answers

- **Does pump current truly measure pressure?** It is a configurable pump-load hypothesis. The included fit script and proposed ground-truth transducer protocol are for identifying the line on a specific controller and pump.
- **Are the accuracy numbers real?** No; all performance results are generated by this simulator. They measure internal consistency only.
- **Can you identify which cartridge is clogged?** RO permeability is the most directly normalized signal. Sediment and UF share hydraulic effects; current and flow alone may not separate them.
- **Is the filter still safe to drink from?** The app never certifies safety. It only supports maintenance planning.
- **What comes next?** Collect labelled staged-clogging and run-to-failure bench trajectories across controller modes and challenge waters, then freeze an external validation protocol.
